What Is a Deadlift 1RM?

Your deadlift 1RM is the amount of weight you could theoretically lift for one repetition. An estimated 1RM can help you monitor changes in strength without always testing a true maximum.

How Is It Calculated?

The calculator uses the Epley formula.

1RM = Weight × (1 + Reps ÷ 30)

Example

If you deadlift 100 kg for 5 repetitions:


100 × (1 + 5 ÷ 30)


Estimated 1RM ≈ 116.7 kg

Why Track Your Deadlift 1RM?

  • Track strength progression over time.
  • Estimate training weights.
  • Keep a history of previous performances.
  • Compare your strength between training periods.

Important Note

The result is an estimate rather than an exact measurement. Actual performance can vary depending on technique, fatigue, experience and other factors.
