What Is a Squat 1RM?

A squat 1RM represents the maximum weight you could theoretically lift for one repetition. Because testing a true maximum can be demanding, an estimated 1RM can provide another way to track strength.

Squat 1RM Formula

This website uses the Epley formula.

1RM = Weight × (1 + Reps ÷ 30)

Example

Suppose you squat 80 kg for 8 repetitions.


80 × (1 + 8 ÷ 30)


Estimated 1RM ≈ 101.3 kg

Benefits of Tracking Squat 1RM

  • Monitor strength changes over time.
  • Estimate training loads from your current strength.
  • Keep a record of previous workouts.
  • Compare performance across different training periods.

Remember

An estimated 1RM is not a guarantee of your actual maximum strength. Results can vary based on technique, fatigue, training experience and other conditions.
