What Is a Bench Press 1RM?

Your bench press 1RM, or one rep max, is the maximum amount of weight you can theoretically lift for one repetition. An estimated 1RM can be useful for tracking strength and comparing training performance over time.

How Is Bench Press 1RM Calculated?

Our calculator uses the Epley formula to estimate your one rep max from a submaximal set.

1RM = Weight × (1 + Reps ÷ 30)

Example

If you bench press 60 kg for 10 repetitions:


60 × (1 + 10 ÷ 30)


Estimated 1RM ≈ 80 kg

Why Calculate Your Bench Press 1RM?

  • Track your strength progress.
  • Estimate your maximum strength without testing a true maximum.
  • Calculate training weights based on percentages.
  • Compare your performance between different training periods.

Important Note

A calculated 1RM is only an estimate. Your actual maximum can vary depending on technique, fatigue, experience, equipment and other factors.
