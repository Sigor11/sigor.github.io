What is a 1RM?

Your one-repetition maximum, commonly called 1RM, is the maximum amount of weight you can theoretically lift for one repetition of an exercise.

Testing your true maximum can be physically demanding. A 1RM calculator estimates your maximum strength using a weight you can already lift for multiple repetitions.

How does the calculator work?

This calculator uses the Epley formula to estimate your one-repetition maximum.

Estimated 1RM = Weight × (1 + Reps ÷ 30)

For example, if you bench press 80 kg for 8 repetitions, the estimated 1RM is approximately 101.3 kg.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the calculated 1RM exact?

No. It is an estimate based on your repetitions and weight. Your actual 1RM may be different depending on technique, experience, fatigue, and other factors.

Which exercises can I use?

The calculator currently supports Bench Press, Squat, and Deadlift.

What percentage should I train with?

Training intensity depends on your goal and experience. The calculator provides several percentage ranges as a reference rather than a personalized training prescription.

Can beginners use this calculator?

Yes. However, beginners should prioritize proper technique and safe training practices rather than attempting maximal lifts.
