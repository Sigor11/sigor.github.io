What is 1RM?

1RM stands for One Rep Max. It is the maximum amount of weight you can theoretically lift for one repetition with proper form. Your 1RM can be useful for estimating strength and planning training weights.

How Does the Calculator Work?

This calculator uses the Epley formula to estimate your one rep max.


1RM = Weight × (1 + Reps ÷ 30)


Example: If you bench press 60 kg for 10 repetitions, the estimated 1RM is approximately 80 kg.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the calculated 1RM exact?

No. It is an estimate based on your weight and repetitions. Your actual one rep max may be different.

Which exercises can I calculate?

The calculator currently supports Bench Press, Squat and Deadlift.

Where are my workout records stored?

Your workout history is stored locally in your browser. The records are not uploaded to our server.

Can I use this calculator on my phone?

Yes. The website is designed to work on both desktop and mobile devices.
