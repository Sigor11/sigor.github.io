What Does 1RM Percentage Mean?

A 1RM percentage represents a portion of your estimated one rep max. For example, if your estimated 1RM is 100 kg, then 80% of your 1RM would be 80 kg.

Training Weight = 1RM × Percentage

Why Use 1RM Percentages?

Percentages provide a simple way to describe training loads relative to your current estimated strength. They can also make it easier to compare training sessions when your strength changes.

Example

Estimated 1RM: 120 kg

70% = 84 kg

80% = 96 kg

90% = 108 kg

95% = 114 kg

Are 1RM Percentages Exact?

No. Training performance can change depending on fatigue, sleep, technique, experience and other factors. A percentage is best viewed as a useful reference rather than a guarantee of how many repetitions someone can perform.
